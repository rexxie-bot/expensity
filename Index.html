FAQ

Questions people ask first

What is Expensity?

Expensity is an expense tracker. You log what you spend, sort it into categories, set budgets and review reports.

Who is Expensity for?

Anyone who wants to see their spending clearly: individuals, households, freelancers and small teams.

What kind of expenses can I track?

Cash, card and transfer spending in any category. You can also record recurring bills and tag expenses by trip or project.

Can I use more than one currency?

Yes. Each expense keeps its own currency, and reports can be shown in the currency you pick.

How do I add expenses?

Type the amount on the home screen, choose a category, and save. Attach a receipt photo if you have one.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Download any date range as a spreadsheet or PDF report.
